Version 1.0 is the first release of Stakiz.

• Introduction pages "Collar chores", "Scan, then snap", and "Clip the day", with "Skip", "Next", and "Continue".
• A day board of "Today's posts", a tray of chore chips, "Snap", "Collars", "Scan collar", "Undo seat", and "Clipped days".
• "Pets" with "Pet name", "Add pet", and "Print collar sheet".
• "Chores" with "Pet", "Chore name", "Time window", "Add chore", and "Active today".
• "Scan collar" reads a square code. "Known collars" can be tapped with no camera.
• "Undo seat" lifts the last chore seated today and reopens a clipped day.
• "Log" for the last seven days, "Misses", and "Export week".
• "Goals" with "Streak", "Today", and "Yesterday".
• "Settings" with "Show intro again", "Reset household", "Contact support", and "Open Settings".
• The household remains on this device. A new calendar day starts a new open day. Yesterday's seats stay in "Log".
• A Watch app shows "Snap Walk", the figure "1", and "Drag the open chore onto its post." It has no buttons.
