Thank you for reviewing Stakiz again. We retested the build under review on a clean device. The intro, the day board, and every cover completed, and no crash, dead end, empty screen, or non-functioning feature could be reproduced.

Three behaviours are deliberate. A quick pass can mistake each one for a stuck control.

1. "Add chore" stays off until "Chore name" contains a character other than spaces and a pet is selected. If the form shows "Add a pet first.", it does not offer "Add chore". Open "Pets", type a "Pet name", tap "Add pet", return, choose "Pet", type a name, and "Add chore" enables.
2. A tray chip dragged before any collar has been read does not seat. The status becomes "Scan a collar, then drag the chore." Read a collar, then drop the chip on its own glowing post.
3. "Show" on a held row only marks that chore. It does not seat it. The status becomes that chore's name plus " is marked on today. Drag it onto its post." Drag it to the glowing post, or tap "Snap " plus the chore name.

Full review path. No account, no login:
- A cold launch with no saved household opens a three-page intro. There is no separate splash. Page dots mark the page. "Skip" ends the intro. Page 1: "Collar chores", "Each pet keeps today's chores on one paper column.", "Next". Page 2: "Scan, then snap", "Read a collar, then drag the chore onto its post.", "Next". Page 3: "Clip the day", "When every chore is seated, the day clips shut.", "Continue". "Continue" or "Skip" opens the day board.
- The board has no navigation-bar title and no tab bar. Across the top: "Pets", "Chores", "Log", "Goals", "Settings". "Close" on each returns to the board.
- Empty board: headline "No chores yet", status "Household empty. Add a pet.", plus "No chores yet.", "Add a pet and a chore.", and "Add a pet".
- "Pets": placeholder "Pet name". "Add pet" enables once the name has a non-space character. "Done" dismisses the keyboard. A collar code shows under the pet. "Print collar sheet" opens a share sheet whose control reads "Share collar sheet" and whose preview title is "Collar sheet".
- "Chores": "Pet", "Chore name", and "Time window" (starts as "Morning"; a blank window is stored as "Today"). Saved rows show "Chore name", the pet, the window, and "Active today". Turning that switch off removes the chore from today's posts.
- "Today's posts" lists active chores. A glowing post shows the pet, a middle dot, the window, the chore, and "Glowing post". An open row shows "Open". A seated row shows "Seated". The tray caption changes with the day. Under "Collars", a pet-name button counts as a scan. Before the camera is allowed, the button is "Continue" and the line is "Reads a collar code on this device."
- "Scan collar" (disabled once the day is clipped) opens heading "Scan a collar" and the same status line as the board. "Known collars" lists the pet name, a space, and the collar code. A tap reads that code and closes. An unknown code shows "Scan failed. Try again." No pets shows "Add a pet before scanning."
- Seat by a matching drag, a tap on the glowing or open row, or "Snap " plus the chore name. A matching post keeps the chore. A wrong post shows "Wrong post. It bounced back." A miss shows "Missed the post. Try again." A filled post shows "That post already holds a chore."
- When every active chore is seated, the headline is "Day clipped" and the status is "Day clipped." The tray reads "Clipped. Undo the last seat to reopen." "Undo seat" is then the stronger button. It lifts the last seat and reopens a clipped day. With nothing seated it stays disabled; a tap with nothing to lift shows "No seat to undo." "Clipped days" shows the streak and today's date number.
- "Log": empty state "No seats this week.", "Snap a chore on today.", and "Show today". With history, body title "Week", header "Chore" and seven day numbers. Filled checks are seated; open circles are open.
